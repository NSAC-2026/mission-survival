How a Stable Orbit is Made

SCIENCE TIP

A stable orbit happens when forward velocity matches Earth’s gravity perfectly! The satellite falls towards Earth but never hits it.

🛸 HOW AN ORBIT IS MADE

An orbit is a continuous falling balancing act! Gravity is constantly pulling the satellite down toward Earth, but the satellite is moving sideways so fast that it keeps missing the planet. As long as the forward speed (velocity) perfectly matches the gravity pull, the satellite stays locked in a beautiful circular path forever!
